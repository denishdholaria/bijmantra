## The Problem We Share

Agricultural research generates extraordinary data — phenotypic observations, genomic profiles, multi-environment trial results, germplasm records, environmental measurements, seed operations logs. Yet in most institutions, this data lives in silos: spreadsheets, disconnected databases, proprietary software, and institutional systems that do not talk to each other.

The cost of this fragmentation is real. Breeding decisions get made without the full picture. Cross-domain insights — the kind that connect a genomic signal to a field observation to a climate pattern — require manual reconciliation that takes weeks instead of hours. Institutional knowledge walks out the door when researchers move on, because it was never systematically captured.

This is not a new problem. But it remains largely unsolved for the institutions that need it most.

---

## What BijMantra Is

BijMantra (https://bijmantra.org) is an open-source, cross-domain agricultural intelligence platform. It is designed to unify plant breeding, germplasm management, field trials, genomics, seed operations, and environmental data into a single, institution-controlled system.

At its core is **REEVU** — Reason → Evidence → Evaluation → Validation → Unit — a scientifically deterministic AI reasoning system built specifically for agricultural decision-making. REEVU does not guess. Every conclusion it draws traces back to specific data records, with explicit evidence chains and uncertainty acknowledgment. It is designed to think the way a careful researcher thinks: connecting breeding strategy to phenotypic observations to genomic predictions to environmental context, with full provenance at every step.

The platform is built on open standards. It implements **BrAPI v2.1**, which means it is designed to work alongside the tools your institution already uses — BreedBase, Gigwa, FieldBook, and others. It runs on your own servers. You own your data. There is no vendor lock-in, no cloud dependency, no subscription.

The technical stack is deliberate: Python and FastAPI for orchestration, React and TypeScript for the interface, Rust and WebAssembly for performance-critical calculations, and Fortran for validated numerical methods where scientific provenance matters. This is not a prototype built for a demo. It is infrastructure built to last.

## Where I Am, Honestly

I am a solo developer. BijMantra has substantial foundational work complete — the full-stack platform, the BrAPI interoperability layer, the core breeding and trials modules, the REEVU reasoning system in its early form. But it is far from the research-grade.

The gap between a working system and a trustworthy research tool has software engineering problem, but it is a domain validation problem. I can build the architecture. I cannot, alone, ensure that the statistical methods are appropriate for your breeding program, that the reasoning patterns reflect how researchers actually think, or that the workflows map to the real complexity of institutional agricultural science.

That is why I am writing this letter.

---

## What I Am Asking For

My first ask is **scientific partnership** — the domain expertise and real-world validation that no amount of money can substitute for at this stage. If I had the funds to hire experts, I still could not buy the institutional credibility, the genuine research workflows, or the honest critical feedback that comes from researchers who have skin in the game.

That said, I will be direct: funding is also welcome. BijMantra is being built on personal time and personal resources. Financial support — whether from institutions, research grants, foundations, or agricultural industry partners — would accelerate development, allow me to dedicate more time to the platform, and make it possible to bring in additional expertise. If your institution sees value in what BijMantra is building and wants to support it financially, that conversation is absolutely open.

But collaboration comes first. A funded platform that does not solve real problems is worthless. A validated platform with institutional backing is a strong foundation for securing the resources to grow.

Specifically, I am looking for:

**Research institutions and universities** willing to pilot BijMantra in real workflows — a breeding program, a trial series, a germplasm collection. Three to six months of active use, honest feedback, and the willingness to tell me what is wrong.

**Domain experts** — plant breeders, agronomists, quantitative geneticists, bioinformaticians — willing to review whether REEVU's analytical methods are scientifically sound. Whether the statistical approaches (ANOVA, BLUP, GxE analysis, genomic prediction) are correctly implemented and appropriately applied. Whether the cross-domain reasoning reflects how researchers actually work.

**Institutions with existing data infrastructure** willing to test BrAPI interoperability — to find out where the integration works and where it breaks.

**Graduate students and research groups** interested in contributing to an open-source platform as part of their research, or in using BijMantra as infrastructure for their own work.

## What BijMantra Offers in Return

- A **perpetual, royalty-free institutional license** to deploy BijMantra on your own servers for research and education, with no restrictions
- **Custom feature development** — if your research program needs capabilities that BijMantra does not yet have, I am open to building them. This is not a fixed product. If a specific analytical workflow, data integration, or domain module would make BijMantra genuinely useful for your work, that is exactly the kind of requirement I want to hear. Research-driven feature requests will be treated as first-class development priorities, not a backlog item
- **Feature prioritization** — beyond custom work, research needs identified through collaboration go to the top of the development roadmap
- **Recognition** as a research collaborator on bijmantra.org, with your institutional affiliation and a description of the partnership
- **Co-authorship** on any publications that emerge from collaborative validation work, platform studies, or methodological papers
- **Early access** to new capabilities before public release

I cannot offer financial compensation at this stage. I can offer transparency, responsiveness, and the commitment that your institution's scientific needs will directly shape what this platform becomes. And if collaboration leads to grant opportunities or joint funding applications, I am fully open to pursuing those together.

## Why This Matters Beyond BijMantra

Agricultural research institutions are under increasing pressure — to accelerate breeding cycles, to integrate climate data into decision-making, to demonstrate impact with limited resources. The tools available are often proprietary, expensive, and built for commercial breeding programs rather than research institutions.

Open-source agricultural intelligence infrastructure, built to research-grade standards and validated by the scientific community, could change that. Not just for BijMantra's users, but as a model for how agricultural data systems can be built — openly, rigorously, and in service of science rather than vendor interests.

That is the larger ambition. But it starts with honest collaboration between a developer who can build the system and researchers who know whether it is actually useful.
